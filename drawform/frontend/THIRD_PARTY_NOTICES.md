The original VEYRA release used Space Grotesk through Google Fonts. This DrawForm adaptation replaces that import with system Chinese text and a locally hosted UI subset of Maoken Glitch Sans (猫啃网故障黑). Maoken and NightFurySL2001 released the font under SIL Open Font License 1.1, based on Adobe Source Han Sans. The subset keeps the glyph outlines and copyright and uses a new family name. See `public/assets/fonts/maoken/` for OFL, copyright, exact source commit/version/hash and the shipped subset. The official font introduction is https://www.maoken.com/freefonts/14318.html . The source mirror identifies itself as Version 1.000; it is not claimed to be the official site's current 1.01 release.
